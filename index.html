YOUR NEXT STEP

Cybersecurity Readiness Questionnaire

Answer the following questions to receive preliminary cybersecurity recommendations based on your organization's current practices.

Section 1: Govern (GV)

This section helps identify who manages cybersecurity and makes important security decisions within your organization.

Section 2: Identify (ID)

This section helps determine which systems and information are most important to your organization's daily operations.

Section 3: Protect (PR)

This section evaluates the basic security measures your organization uses to protect accounts, employees, and data.

Section 4: Detect (DE)

This section helps determine whether your organization can recognize suspicious activity before it causes more damage.

Section 5: Respond (RS)

This section identifies whether your organization knows whom to contact and what actions to take when a cybersecurity incident occurs

Section 6: Recover (RC)

This section evaluates whether your organization can restore important systems and continue operations after a cybersecurity incident.
